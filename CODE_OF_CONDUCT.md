# Collaboration and conduct

People collaborating in this GitHub workspace are expected to treat each other with respect and professionalism, welcome diverse perspectives, avoid discriminatory or harassing behaviour, and give constructive feedback.

Humanitarian principles and safeguarding matter in both our products and the way we work. Protect the privacy and dignity of communities, volunteers, personnel and partners. Never use code reviews, issues or discussions to disclose protected information or target individuals.

Raise conduct or safeguarding concerns confidentially through the existing approved Lebanese Red Cross internal reporting channels, not through a public issue. This repository guideline does not supersede formal institutional conduct, safeguarding or disciplinary procedures.
